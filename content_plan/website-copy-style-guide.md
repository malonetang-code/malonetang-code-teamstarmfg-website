# Teamstar Website Copy Style Guide

Updated: 2026-07-17

## Positioning

The website presents Teamstar Manufacturing as a custom industrial machine-knife manufacturer. Public copy should help a technical buyer identify the applicable product family, understand the manufacturing and inspection capabilities, and submit usable RFQ information.

## Benchmark Findings

The review covered official websites from DIENES, TKM, Baucor, York Saw & Knife, Fernite, LUTZ BLADES and Winterhoff Picard. The recurring structure is:

1. Products organized by industry, equipment or cutting application
2. Manufacturing capabilities described through processes and control points
3. Quality information tied to material, in-process and final inspection
4. RFQ entry based on drawings, physical samples, machine information and application conditions
5. Technical claims stated only when supported by an identified specification or certificate

## Tone

- Technical, factual and concise
- Formal business Chinese and natural industrial English
- Direct statements instead of conversational prompts
- Specific process language instead of unsupported superlatives
- No invented tolerances, hardness ranges, capacity, lead times or service-life results

## Heading Rules

- Headings, navigation labels, card titles and page titles use no terminal punctuation
- Headings use noun phrases or direct category names
- Do not use conversational structures such as "把...", "先...", "从你...开始" or rhetorical questions
- Use one primary concept per heading
- Reserve full sentences and sentence punctuation for body copy

## Preferred English Terms

- industrial machine knives
- custom industrial blades
- drawing-based manufacture
- physical-sample replication
- application review
- technical requirement review
- process planning
- prototype validation
- series production
- in-process inspection
- final inspection
- batch release and batch records

Avoid literal translations from Chinese, conversational second-person copy and marketing claims that are not supported by published evidence.

## Content Pattern

Product page:

1. Product family and typical equipment
2. Application and key technical considerations
3. Technical review and manufacturing process
4. RFQ information required

Capability page:

1. Process scope
2. Equipment or documented capability
3. Applicable control requirement
4. Drawing-specific qualification statement

Quality page:

1. Technical and material review
2. In-process inspection
3. Final inspection
4. Batch release and applicable records

## Evidence Boundary

Public claims must be traceable to approved company materials, certificates, equipment photographs or confirmed production facts. Project-specific hardness, tolerance, material, lead time and expected service life are stated only after technical review.
